Scenario

Priya Nair is building a daily learning habit and wants to see how one freeze protects her streak.

Try this

  1. Choose a time below to move Priya's demo clock.
  2. Complete the lesson and submit the checkpoint on that day.
  3. Move the clock again and repeat to see the streak rules in action.

You should see

  • Same day, evening: XP increases, while the streak stays at 4.
  • Next day: the streak becomes 5 and the freeze remains available.
  • Skip a day: the freeze covers the gap, the streak extends, and freezes go from 1 to 0.
  • Skip again with no freeze: the streak resets to 1.
